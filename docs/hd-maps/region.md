# Identifying a region of interest

The Region of interest tool allows you to define specific regions and identify the similar regions by color. The types of region of interest for the vehicle are defined by the software engineering teams. They define the types of region of interest according to their needs.

## Creating a region of interest { #createedge }

To create a region of interest:

1.  In the tools bar, click on the **Region of interest** icon ![edge](../images/interest.svg).

    **Result:** A small square hovers with the pointer in the map window. The square represents one point of the region of interest.

2.  Click to place the points of the region of interest.

After you have created a region of interest, you can identify similar regions by color. For more information, see [Identifying similar regions of interest](#identify).

## Identifying similar regions of interest { #identify }

To define similar regions of interest, you need to use the same name prefix. The regions of interest with the same name prefix are in the same color.

To identify similar regions of interest:

1.  In the tool bar, click on the **HDMap Selection** icon ![HD map selection](../images/hdmapselection.svg).
2.  Select the region of interest you have created.
3.  In the **Region of Interest** section at the bottom of the left pane,
4.  In the **Description** field, enter a name with the following naming convention: prefix\_name. The regions of interest are sorted by the prefix.

!!! example

    you want to create regions of interest to identify some buildings. The names of the regions of interest could be "building\_high", "building\_medium" and "building\_small". All the regions of interest with the prefix "building" are sorted in the same category and colored in the same color.

In the **Elements** left pane, you can view or hide the regions of interest you have created.
